Hair Transplant Cost in Istanbul: What Sets the Price and What the Package Should Include

Beautifulist Clinic team · November 2026

One of the questions we hear most is: how much does a hair transplant cost in Istanbul? The honest answer is that the price varies from person to person — but understanding what drives it helps you compare offers wisely and avoid surprises.

Why Istanbul became a hair transplant destination

Istanbul combines long experience in this field, modern facilities and lower living and operating costs than Europe and the Gulf, plus direct flights from most major cities.

What determines the price

  1. Technique: Sapphire FUE, DHI or a combination.
  2. Size of the area: light thickening differs from a large bald area.
  3. What the package includes: hotel, transfers, medication and follow-up.
  4. Length of stay and hotel type.
  5. Extra services: such as PRP sessions, beard or eyebrow transplant.

What an all-inclusive package should include

  • Airport pick-up and drop-off.
  • Hotel accommodation during treatment.
  • All transfers between hotel and clinic.
  • Pre-operative blood tests.
  • Medication and aftercare products (shampoo and lotion).
  • The first wash at the clinic.
  • A translator / companion who speaks your language.
  • Follow-up after you return home.

Watch out for these points

A very low price may mean the procedure is not done in a licensed clinic, or that essential services are not included and will be added later. Always ask for the package details in writing before booking, and ask: who performs each stage? What exactly is included? How does follow-up work after you travel?
