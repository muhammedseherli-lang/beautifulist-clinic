Hair Transplant Aftercare Day by Day: The First Weeks and the Months That Follow

Beautifulist Clinic team · November 2026

The success of a hair transplant does not end on procedure day — how you care for the grafts in the first weeks plays a big role in the result. Here is what to expect and what to do at each stage.

Days 1–3

  • Sleep on your back with your head slightly raised (two pillows or a neck pillow) to reduce swelling.
  • Do not touch or scratch the transplanted area.
  • Avoid bending over and lifting heavy objects.
  • Take your medication exactly as the team explained.
  • Mild swelling on the forehead around day 3 is common and settles within a few days.

Day 3: the first wash

Your first wash is done at the clinic, where we show you the correct method: apply the lotion, then a light shampoo lather pressed gently with your fingertips — no rubbing — then rinse gently with lukewarm water.

Days 4–14

  • Wash your head daily the same way.
  • Small scabs form around the grafts and gradually fall off with washing by day 10–14. Do not pick them.
  • Avoid direct sun, swimming, sauna and strenuous exercise.
  • A loose hat that does not press on the grafts can be worn after the first days, as advised by the team.

Weeks 2–8: shock loss

During this period much of the transplanted hair sheds. This is completely normal — the follicle stays under the skin and prepares to grow new hair.

Months 3–18

  • Months 3–4: new hair starts to appear, fine at first.
  • Months 6–8: clear improvement in density and thickness.
  • Months 12–18: the final result is complete.

When can you return to normal life?

Office work: after about 7–10 days. Light exercise: after two weeks. Heavy exercise and swimming: after one month. Scissor haircut: after one month; clippers on the transplanted area: after 2–3 months.
