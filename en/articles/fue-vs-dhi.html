Sapphire FUE vs DHI: Which Hair Transplant Technique Is Right for You?

Beautifulist Clinic team · November 2026

When you start researching hair transplants in Istanbul, two names come up everywhere: Sapphire FUE and DHI. Both are modern, effective techniques. The question is not which one is "best" in general, but which one suits your case.

What the two techniques have in common

In both, grafts are extracted one by one from the donor area (the back and sides of the head) with a fine tool — no strip, no stitches. The real difference is in the implantation stage: how the grafts are placed into the recipient area.

Sapphire FUE

Tiny channels are opened in the recipient area with sapphire blades, then the grafts are placed into them. The smooth sapphire edges create smaller, cleaner incisions, which supports faster healing.

  • Ideal for large bald areas.
  • Allows a high number of grafts in one session.
  • Usually requires shaving the whole head.

DHI (Choi pen)

Each graft is loaded into a Choi pen, which opens the channel and implants the graft in a single step. This gives very precise control over the angle and direction of each hair.

  • Ideal for adding density where hair still exists.
  • Great for designing a precise frontal hairline.
  • Sometimes possible without fully shaving the head.

How to choose

If you have a wide bald area on top, Sapphire FUE is usually the practical choice. If the goal is to thicken thinning hair or redraw a natural hairline, DHI may fit better. In many cases both techniques are combined in the same procedure.
