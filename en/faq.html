Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear most before a hair transplant. Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

Is a hair transplant painful?
The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so you do not feel pain during the transplant. The only noticeable sensation is the anaesthetic injections at the start; afterwards a simple painkiller for a few days is usually enough.
How long does the procedure take?
Usually 6–8 hours depending on the number of grafts and the technique, with breaks and lunch during the day.
How many days do I need to stay in Istanbul?
Usually 3 days: arrival day, procedure day, then check-up and first wash before you fly home.
When will I see the final result?
New hair starts to appear around months 3–4, the result is clearly visible after 6–8 months, and the final result is complete between 12 and 18 months.
Is it normal for transplanted hair to fall out after a few weeks?
Yes. Between weeks 2 and 8 much of the transplanted hair sheds (known as shock loss), while the follicles stay in place and later grow new hair.
Is a hair transplant permanent?
Grafts are taken from the back of the head, which is resistant to hair loss, so transplanted hair usually lasts for life. Your original non-transplanted hair may continue to thin over time, which is why we plan with the future in mind.
How many grafts do I need?
It depends on your degree of hair loss and the density of your donor area. Send us clear photos of your head on WhatsApp and we will send a free graft estimate.
When can I go back to work?
Most people return to office work after 7–10 days. Physically demanding work or heavy sweating should wait two weeks to a month.
Can women have a hair transplant?
Yes, in many cases such as thinning along the parting or a high frontal hairline. Your photos and assessment show whether a transplant is the right solution.
Do I need a visa for Turkey?
It depends on your nationality; many countries are visa-exempt and others can get an e-Visa easily. Please check Turkey's official e-Visa website — our team is happy to help.
How do I book?
Message us on WhatsApp, send your photos and receive your plan. Once you choose a date, we confirm your booking and arrange your transfer and hotel.
