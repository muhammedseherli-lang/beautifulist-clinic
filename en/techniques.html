Hair Transplant Techniques

We choose the right technique after assessing your photos — sometimes combining more than one in the same procedure.

Sapphire FUE Hair Transplant

Grafts are extracted one by one from the donor area, then fine channels are opened with sapphire blades and the grafts are implanted. Smaller incisions mean faster healing and higher density over large areas.

Suitable for

  • Large bald areas at the front and top
  • Those who need many grafts in one session
  • Those happy to shave their head fully
  • Fast recovery with no stitches or linear scar

DHI Hair Transplant

Each graft is loaded into a Choi pen, which opens the channel and implants the graft in one step, with precise control over angle, direction and depth.

Suitable for

  • Adding density without harming existing hair
  • Designing a precise, natural frontal hairline
  • Those who prefer not to shave fully
  • Women with thinning hair

Beard Transplant

Grafts are placed at angles that follow your natural beard growth, to fill gaps or thicken the beard and moustache and create balanced facial lines.

Suitable for

  • Thin or patchy beards
  • Gaps on the cheeks, chin or moustache
  • Covering scars in the beard area
  • Sharper, more defined beard lines

Eyebrow Transplant

A precise transplant of a small number of grafts shaped to your face, for a permanent, natural result instead of drawing or tattooing.

Suitable for

  • Thin or uneven eyebrows
  • Eyebrow loss from over-plucking or scars
  • Men and women alike
  • Those who want to stop drawing their brows daily
