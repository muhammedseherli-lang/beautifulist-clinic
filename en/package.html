Your itinerary

3 days in Istanbul

Day 1

Arrival & welcome

Our team meets you at the airport and takes you to your hotel. We go over the next day's plan and answer your questions.

Day 2

Procedure day

Tests, hairline design and final agreement, then the procedure under local anaesthesia (6–8 hours with breaks and lunch). Afterwards you return to the hotel to rest.

Day 3

Check-up, first wash & departure

Check-up of the transplanted area, first wash at the clinic with aftercare instructions, medication and aftercare kit handed over, then transfer to the airport.

Before you travel

  • Send your photos, get your plan and confirm your booking.
  • Tell us about any medication you take or chronic conditions.
  • Stop smoking and alcohol a week before the procedure if possible.
  • Avoid aspirin and blood thinners unless you have checked with the team.

What to bring

  • Passport and return ticket.
  • A button-up shirt that is easy to wear after the procedure.
  • A loose hat that does not press on your head.
  • A neck pillow for the first nights and your flight home.
